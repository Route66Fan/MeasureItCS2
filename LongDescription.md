Measure It! for CS2 lets you place a chain of points anywhere in your city and get instant elevation, distance, slope, and direction readouts between them — a straight port of the classic Measure It! mod, rebuilt for Cities: Skylines II.

FEATURES

- Multi-point measurement chains. Click as many points as you like; each segment gets its own row in the readout panel, plus running totals for the whole chain.
- Choose your units: Meters, Kilometers, Feet, or Miles for distance; Percentage or Degrees for slope; Degrees or Cardinal (N/NE/E/...) for direction.
- Draggable readout panel that remembers its position between uses.
- Customizable point and line color.
- Numbered point markers on the map that match the segment table.
- Works on elevated bridges and inside tunnels, not just flat ground.
- Rebindable keyboard shortcuts.
- Toolbar icon in the game's Universal Mod Menu.

HOW TO USE

1. Click the ruler icon in the Universal Mod Menu, or press Ctrl+M, to activate the tool.
2. Left-click on the map to place a point. Keep clicking to build a chain.
3. Right-click (or Ctrl+X) to undo the last point.
4. Adjust units, point color, and keybinds from the panel or from Options -> Measure It! for CS2.
