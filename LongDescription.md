Ported from the original CS1 mod using Claude AI (Anthropic).
Original CS1 mod can be found here: https://github.com/keallu/CSL-MeasureIt

Measure It! for CS2 lets you place a chain of points anywhere in your city and get instant elevation, distance, slope, and direction readouts between them - a port of the classic Measure It! mod, rebuilt for Cities: Skylines II.

FEATURES:

- Place multiple measurement points; each segment gets its own row in the mod window, plus running totals for the whole chain.
- Choose your units: Meters, Kilometers, Feet, or Miles for distance; Percentage or Degrees for slope; Degrees or Cardinal (N/NE/E/...) for direction.
- Draggable mod window that remembers where you put it, separately for cities and the editor (Options -> Reset Panel Position puts it back in the center).
- Change the color of points and lines in the mod's options menu (Yellow, Magenta, Red, Green, Cyan, White, or Orange).
- Numbered point markers on the map that match the segment table in the mod window.
- Works on elevated bridges and inside tunnels, not just flat ground.
- Snap to nodes: clicks snap to actual road, pipe, track, and subway nodes - measure precisely to/through buried pipes and underground lines, not just visible surfaces. Turn it on or off with the checkbox in the mod window.
- Connecting lines follow the real network route between two snapped points (tracing the actual curve of the road, pipe, or subway line) instead of just cutting a straight line between them. The numbers in the window are always the straight-line measurement between the two points; the route line is a visual aid.
- Works in the editor too. The editor has no Universal Mod Menu, so toggle the tool there with Ctrl+M.
- Rebindable keyboard shortcuts (defaults: Ctrl+M toggle tool, Ctrl+Z add point at cursor, Ctrl+X undo last point).
- Toolbar icon is located in the game's Universal Mod Menu (in-game only).

HOW TO USE:

1. Click the ruler icon in the Universal Mod Menu, or press Ctrl+M, to activate the tool. In the editor, press Ctrl+M.
2. Left-click on the map to place a point. Keep clicking to build a chain.
3. Right-click (or Ctrl+X) to undo the last point.
4. Adjust point color and keybinds from Options -> Measure It! for CS2.
5. Adjust units and snap-to-nodes from the mod window.
